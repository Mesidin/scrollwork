# The House

You start on the **front porch**. North is the foyer.

Marta the maid will talk. Try `ask maid about key`, `ask maid about cellar`, `ask maid about lamp`.

The parlor (north of the foyer) has a brass key and a coat. The kitchen (east) has a lamp — `use lamp` before you go downstairs. The study (west) has a portrait worth examining.

The cellar door is **down** from the foyer. It is locked. Unlock it, open it, take a light with you. East is wine and a rat. West is the cistern.

`steady` spends Calm to settle your Grit. Careful characters start with more Calm; Reckless characters start with more Grit.
